About eHospital

eHospital is an education platform for clinical training. Faculty organize interns into teams, build rotations from a sequence of prompts and supporting media, and review the feedback interns share along the way.

Mission

Give every intern a structured, well-supported rotation, and give faculty a clear view of how their interns are progressing.

How it works

Faculty build a sequence of prompts, each with a file, link, discussion board or quiz attached. Students work through the prompts in order, and faculty review the resulting feedback by thread, intern or keyword.
